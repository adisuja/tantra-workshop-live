The strategic thesis

Own the whole journey

If a user doesn't understand or use Tantra, that is our fault, not theirs. The funnel must carry them from cold traffic all the way to active, paying, retained usage.

The ClickFunnels model

Launch via services; use them as a paid feedback loop to build a better product. Front-end must self-liquidate so acquisition costs nothing net.

Differentiate as a workshop

A one-way webinar can't stand out against known players. A 3-day workshop where attendees do and get something daily converts far better — and the recordings become the course.

Retention is the metric

Every element is designed so that as many attendees as possible keep paying for support and stay retained on Tantra month after month.

Three days, locked. Day 1 Wed Sep 30, 11am ET, then Thu Oct 1 and Fri Oct 2 — three days in a row. The cohort moved from Sep 24 on 24 Sep (owner decision). Operations

The delivery engine

A recurring agentic pipeline turns each day's survey answers into a personalized output. Built in Claude Code by Bhaskar Dev.

Capture

Custom full-screen survey (not Typeform) on the landing page. Attendee watches Zoom on one screen, fills the form on the other. Sample answers shown for guidance.

Process

Post-session, a Claude Code agentic workflow reads the day's answers and generates the output using our best models and templates.

Deliver

Rendered as a PDF + live link, pushed via WhatsApp and email reminders — same day, before the next session.

What Bhaskar Dev builds

Interactive survey engine

Full-screen per-day survey + the Funnel Playbook tabbed live interface. Registration + survey data flow to the Tantra Work Tracker.

Agentic output pipeline

Per-day Claude Code workflow, output templates for days 1–5, PDF + live-link generation, WhatsApp + email delivery.

Funnel plumbing

Two workflows (organic / ads), CRM + follow-up sequences (email / WhatsApp / SMS), UTM tracking into GrowthClub OS.

AI personalization

LinkedIn outreach personalization via Tantra, BigHammer-style. Later: introduce the Tantra chat as the knowledge orchestrator.
