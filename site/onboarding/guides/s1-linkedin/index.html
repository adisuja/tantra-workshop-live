LinkedIn Outreach

Connect, follow up and invite through Tantra at a safe pace, plus a company event page.

1The Full Setup
2The Task

Reach named buyers through your own LinkedIn profile without risking the account, and publish a company event that links to your registration page. It runs on Tantra: you sign in to LinkedIn once inside Tantra, and the centre builds the campaign from your verified list and your approved copy. People register on your own page. You're done when the campaign runs at a safe pace, every link carries utm_source=linkedin and LinkedIn registrations show in your CRM with that source.

3Step by Step
  1. 1
    Connect Your LinkedIn Account in Tantra

    You Do This

    Sign in to LinkedIn once inside Tantra. LinkedIn login is the one step nobody can do for you.

    Tantra sends connection requests and messages from your own LinkedIn account.

    Before you start:

    • A LinkedIn profile that reads as a real person: a photo, a headline that states your Day 1 outcome and a real network of connections.
    • No other LinkedIn automation tool running on the same profile.
    1. In Tantra, open Channels and connect LinkedIn.
    2. Sign in, and finish any code LinkedIn asks for.
    3. Let it sync for a few minutes. The account is ready when Tantra shows it running.

    The Connect LinkedIn in Tantra card in your centre checks the connection for you.

  2. 2
    Keep a Safe Daily Pace

    LinkedIn restricts accounts that send too fast, and it does not publish its exact limits. Start low and raise slowly.

    1. Start at about 15 to 20 connection requests a day per account.
    2. Raise it a little each week, only while acceptance stays healthy and LinkedIn shows no warnings.
    3. Limits apply per account across every campaign, not per campaign, so two campaigns from one account share the same daily pace.

    If LinkedIn shows a warning, stop raising and drop back to the last pace that was clean.

  3. 3
    Import One Clean List

    The Centre Does This

    The centre imports everyone on your verified list who has a LinkedIn URL into Tantra.

    Building it by hand instead

    A campaign is only as good as its list. Clean first.

    1. Remove customers, competitors and anyone on your do-not-contact list.
    2. Check that every row has a LinkedIn profile URL, a first name and a company. Rows without a LinkedIn URL can't be reached on LinkedIn; they stay on your email and calendar lists.
    3. One prospect sits on one sender's list only, so nobody hears from two people at your company.

    Example: for a SaaS company, one list of Heads of Operations from the Day 1 prospect list, customers removed.

  4. 4
    Write the Note and the Follow-Ups

    The Centre Does This

    The centre writes the connection note in two versions and the five follow-ups, then builds them into a Tantra campaign that stays paused.

    You Do This

    Approve the copy on the Approve your LinkedIn sequence card.

    Building it by hand instead

    Only people who accept your request get messages. Everyone else is left alone.

    1. One campaign per sender, so each campaign carries one voice.
    2. The connection note holds 300 characters at most. Write it in two versions: A opens with the outcome, B with the objection in your buyer's words.
    3. After they accept, five messages go out at 0, 3, 7, 12 and 25 days.
    4. Use only {FIRST_NAME} and {COMPANY}, typed exactly like that. Every link carries utm_source=linkedin.

    A free LinkedIn account can add a note to only about 5 to 10 requests a month. After that, requests go without the note.

    Example note for a SaaS company: "Sam, saw your team opened a third depot, congrats. I work with Heads of Operations on what the morning replan really costs. Worth connecting?"

    Example note for a consulting firm: "Priya, saw you stepped into the finance lead role, congrats. I help finance leads close the month in days, not weeks. Worth connecting?"

  5. 5
    Give People Two Weeks to Accept

    Someone who accepts on day two should still get the follow-ups, so the window to accept is counted in days, not hours.

    1. Leave a request open for up to 14 days.
    2. After that, withdraw the requests nobody accepted. In LinkedIn itself this is My Network → Manage → Sent, then Withdraw on each one.

    Once a request is withdrawn, LinkedIn blocks a new request to that person for three weeks.

  6. 6
    Start the Campaign

    You Do This

    Approve the launch on the Day 3 Launch one small test run card. Nothing starts until you do.

    The centre leaves the campaign paused. It starts only when you tick LinkedIn on the Day 3 launch card and approve.

    Before you approve:

    1. Read every message in Tantra against your approved copy.
    2. Open every link in a real browser and check the tag is intact.
    3. Check the sender is the right person: the wrong sender puts the wrong voice on every message.

  7. 7
    Publish an Event on Your Company Page

    You Do This

    Create the company event yourself; this is not automated.

    An event gives LinkedIn a second, free path to your registration page. The event drives clicks; the registration itself happens on your page.

    1. On a computer, open your company page as a super admin or content admin. Open Admin view → Create → Create an event, then complete the event form.
    2. Match the name, date, time and time zone to your event platform exactly. The organizer, event type and event format can't be changed after the event is created.
    3. Upload the banner and set the external registration link to your page with ?utm_source=linkedin on the end.
    4. Click Next, write the post text and click Post.
    5. Open the event and click Share, then Invite. Tick the connections and click Invite. Do this in daily batches.

    You can only invite your own 1st-degree connections, and LinkedIn allows each page admin 1,000 invitations a week across all events.

  8. 8
    Count LinkedIn Registrations and Check Daily

    The Centre Does This

    The dashboard syncs your Tantra numbers every hour.

    Building it by hand instead

    An event with an external registration link gives you no attendee emails. People register on your own page, so your registration data is the only list of LinkedIn sign-ups, and the daily numbers show a problem before LinkedIn does.

    1. Count LinkedIn registrations by filtering your registration sheet or CRM to utm_source=linkedin. They already get the join link from your registration page, so there's nothing to import.
    2. Don't report the event's attendee count on LinkedIn as registrations. Only the people on your own list registered.
    3. Each day, read the campaign's numbers: requests sent, accepted and replied. A falling acceptance rate is the first sign the note or the list is wrong.
    4. Anyone who asks to stop goes on your do-not-contact list and out of the campaign in Tantra, the same day.
