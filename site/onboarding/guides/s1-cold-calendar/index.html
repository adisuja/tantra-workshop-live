Cold Calendar

Put the session straight into a prospect's calendar from warmed inboxes, with Tantra and Google.

1The Full Setup
2The Task

Send calendar invites for your session from warmed outreach inboxes, so it appears in the prospect's own calendar. Give Tantra permission through a Google service account, add your inboxes with safe daily limits, import a verified list and run the campaign wizard. You're done when a test invite shows up in a real calendar and the campaign's events × guests matches your list.

3Step by Step
  1. 1
    Create a Google Cloud Project

    Sign in to console.cloud.google.com ↗ with the Google Workspace account that is a super admin of the Workspace holding your sending inboxes. A personal @gmail.com account has no organisation and can't do this.

    1. Open the project picker at the top and click New project.
    2. Name it for the job (for example, Calendar Outreach). In Organisation and Location, pick your domain, not *No organisation*. Click Create.
    3. Open the project picker again and select the new project. Everything in steps 2 and 4 happens inside it.
    4. Open APIs & Services → Library. Search Gmail API, open it and click Enable. Do the same for Google Calendar API. Without both, Tantra's sender check fails later.
  2. 2
    Create the Service Account

    A service account is a robot login that lets Tantra create calendar events for your inboxes.

    1. Open IAM & Admin → Service Accounts and click Create service account.
    2. Name it (for example, Calendar Automation) and click Create and continue.
    3. Skip the two optional parts (Permissions, Principals with access). Delegation needs no project role. Click Done.
    4. Click the new account's email to open it. On the Details tab, copy the Unique ID, a long number. This is the Client ID you paste in step 5.

    If Create service account is blocked, your organisation has *Disable service account creation* switched on. Switch it off the same way as the key policy in step 3.

  3. 3
    Allow Key Creation in Your Organisation

    Google blocks service account keys by default in every organisation created on or after 3 May 2024. Create new key is then greyed out, or fails with iam.disableServiceAccountKeyCreation is enforced. Being the project Owner or a Workspace super admin isn't enough: the fix needs a role that exists only on the organisation, not on the project.

    Part 1: give yourself the role, on the organisation.

    1. Open the project picker at the top and switch from your project to your organisation: the row with the building icon and your domain name. Use the All tab if it isn't listed.
    2. With the organisation selected, open IAM & Admin → IAM and click Grant access.
    3. In New principals, enter your own email. In Select a role, search Organization Policy Administrator and pick it. Check the Resource line at the top of the panel shows your organisation, not a project. Click Save.

    Part 2: switch the policy off.

    1. Still on the organisation, open IAM & Admin → Organization Policies.
    2. In Filter, type Disable service account key creation. Two rows can appear: iam.managed.disableServiceAccountKeyCreation and the older iam.disableServiceAccountKeyCreation. Do the next two items for each one that is enforced.
    3. Click the policy name, then Manage policy. Under Policy source choose Override parent's policy. Click Add a rule, set Enforcement to Off and click Done.
    4. Click Set policy.
    5. Check two more policies in the same list and switch them off the same way if they are enforced: Disable service account creation and Block service account API key bindings.
    6. Switch the project picker back to your project. The change can take a few minutes to apply.

    To open it for this one project only, do Part 1 on the organisation, then do Part 2 with the project selected instead.

    Tip No organisation row in the project picker? You're signed in with an account outside the Workspace, or the project was created under No organisation. Sign in with the Workspace super admin and create the project again under your domain.

  4. 4
    Download the JSON Key
    1. Back in your project, open IAM & Admin → Service Accounts and click the account's email.
    2. Open the Keys tab and click Add key → Create new key.
    3. Choose JSON and click Create. The file downloads once and can't be downloaded again.

    Treat the file as a password: keep it in a password manager, never email it or put it in a repo. If it leaks, delete the key on this tab and make a new one that day.

    Need more sending capacity later? Repeat steps 1 to 5 in a new project. Tantra accepts several service accounts.

  5. 5
    Allow the Service Account to Act for Your Inboxes

    Do this in the Google Workspace that holds your sending inboxes. One entry covers every domain in that Workspace.

    1. Sign in to admin.google.com ↗ as a Super Admin.
    2. Open Security → Access and data control → API controls.
    3. Under Domain-wide delegation, click Manage domain-wide delegation, then Add new.
    4. In Client ID, paste the Unique ID from step 2. Use the number, not the service account's email.
    5. In OAuth scopes, paste these five on one line, separated by commas:

    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile

    1. Click Authorize. It can take minutes, and occasionally up to an hour, to work. Wait before redoing it.

    Also sending cold email from Tantra? Add https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify to the same line. Tantra uses it to tell a real reply from an auto-responder; without it a sender shows as Calendar-only.

  6. 6
    Connect Tantra and Add Your Senders

    You Do This

    Upload the key and add your senders in Tantra yourself; this part is not automated.

    1. Log in to Tantra with Continue with Google. Your first login creates the workspace.
    2. In the sidebar, open Connectors → Google Service Accounts. Drop the JSON key into Upload Service Accounts and click Upload. Nothing can send until this is done.
    3. Open Accounts. In Verify and Add Accounts, paste your sending inbox addresses and click Verify & Add. Each inbox should show Connected. If one shows Disconnected, delegation hasn't spread yet or the Client ID is wrong: wait a few minutes, then click Re-authenticate All.
    4. Set each inbox's limits, or tick several and use Bulk update. Guests/day is the calendar limit: 20 while the inbox is cold, 50 once it has warmed for 2 to 3 weeks. Set Warm to Cold or Warmed to match, then click Save (or Apply to selected). Never leave the default in place.
    5. Connect WebinarGeek. Schedule the session in WebinarGeek so its broadcast exists. Copy your API key from the Advanced section of your WebinarGeek account settings. In Tantra, open Connectors → WebinarGeek, paste it into API Key on the Configuration tab and click Save Configuration; a Connected badge appears. Open the Broadcasts tab and click Sync Broadcasts. If your broadcast isn't listed there, step 10 has nothing to link.

    Tantra spreads a big list over later days rather than go over the limit.

  7. 7
    Use the Same Warmed Domains as Cold Email

    The Centre Does This

    The centre reuses the warmed outreach domains and inboxes from the cold email jobs.

    Building it by hand instead

    Invites go out from real inboxes, so they need the same DNS records and warm-up as cold email: follow steps 3 to 6 of the Cold Email guide. Never use your main brand domain.

  8. 8
    Import Your Contacts

    The Centre Does This

    The centre imports your verified list into Tantra.

    You Do This

    Give the centre your Tantra key.

    Building it by hand instead

    Importing is safe; nothing sends yet.

    1. Open Contacts → Import Contacts and drop in your verified CSV (split it if it's over the limit shown).
    2. Check the email column maps to Email.
    3. Add to a list → Create new list, name it {event_title} - {date}, select it and click Import Contacts.
  9. 9
    Write the Invite Description

    The description is the only copy the prospect reads. Keep it short:

    • The first lines are the promise. Put the join link lower down.
    • Add one line that makes declining easy, so fewer people report it.
    • No em dashes, and no $, % or 'free' in the title.
    • Every link carries utm_source=calendar.

    Keep a copy of the approved text. You'll check the live campaign against it.

  10. 10
    Run the Wizard Carefully: Finishing It Sends

    The Centre Does This

    The centre creates the smart-calendar campaign with a registration link tagged utm_source=calendar and connects a webhook to your funnel.

    You Do This

    Press start once the pre-launch gate is passed.

    Building it by hand instead

    Tantra has no draft. The moment you finish the wizard, the campaign is live, and pausing it doesn't work. Two settings can only be set now, never added later.

    1. Open Campaigns → Create Campaign → Smart Google Calendar.
    2. Name and Tags: name the campaign. Under Link to Webinar Broadcast, pick the WebinarGeek broadcast you synced in step 6 and switch on Register contacts who RSVP yes or maybe, so they get WebinarGeek's join link. The link sits on this first screen: a campaign created without it can never be linked, and its RSVPs get no join link.
    3. Audience: select the list you just imported.
    4. Senders: pick your senders on purpose. Rotate sets between days and check none is already busy today. Tantra splits the list across them in proportion to each one's Guests/day, so pick several rather than loading one inbox. If you create campaigns through the API instead, set distribution to balanced, never fill-first.
    5. Event Details: set the event title, your audience's Time Zone, the start and end time and the join link in Location / Link, then paste the approved description. Open UTM Tracking, tick Enable UTM Parameters and set Campaign Source to calendar.
    6. Review and Launch: check the contact count, event time, sender count and webinar link, and read the description in full.
    7. Only then click Launch Campaign → Yes, Launch Campaign.

    Tip Run the outreach pre-launch gate first. If any box is unticked, don't launch.

  11. 11
    Check It Sent, Then Record It
    1. Open Campaigns → your campaign → ⋮ → View. Check it isn't paused and events × guests matches your list size.
    2. Check the sending inbox's own Google Calendar. Tantra's counters can read zero for a while, and even on campaigns that sent. Never create a second campaign because it looks stuck; people get invited twice.
    3. Export the sent list (Contacts → Export CSV) and write the send date in column A of your master outreach sheet.

    Report registrations from WebinarGeek, not RSVPs.

  12. 12
    Let Every Reply Update Your CRM (Optional, Day 3)

    The Centre Does This

    The webhook does this for you. A yes or maybe registers the contact and sends the welcome, a no marks them declined, a bounce marks them bounced and an unsubscribe turns consent off.

    Building it by hand instead

    Registration already happens through the webinar link from step 10. This automation only keeps your CRM in step: every 60 seconds it reads the sending inboxes, records accepted, tentative and declined replies on the contact, and puts bounces on your do-not-contact list.

    Build it on Day 3 with Claude Code, as a Cloudflare Worker or a GHL workflow, using the prompt below. It needs your CRM's API key and Gmail read access: add https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly to the delegation in step 5.

Build the RSVP and Bounce Reader
Build me a small automation that checks my outreach inboxes every 60 seconds. For each new message: if the subject says the delivery failed, find the recipient in my CRM and mark them bounced (create them if they're missing) and add them to my do-not-contact list. Otherwise read the calendar reply: 'accepted' = yes, 'tentatively accepted' = maybe, 'declined' = no. Update the contact's rsvp_status with source 'Google Calendar Outreach'. Don't register anyone: registration already comes from the webinar linked to the campaign. Skip anything already processed. Show me a test run on three sample messages before it goes live.
