The WhatsApp Twin of Every Email

One short WhatsApp beside each email: a nudge to open it, or the same idea in a few lines.

1The Full Setup
2The Task

Give every newsletter email a WhatsApp twin: either a pointer that makes the reader open the email, or the same idea in a few lines. Twins go only to people who opted in to WhatsApp, as approved message templates, an hour after the email. You're done when your first two twins are approved as templates and scheduled next to their emails.

3Step by Step
  1. 1
    Choose Pointer or Compressed

    Decide per email:

    • Pointer: for long emails (step emails, tool how-tos). Their name, the hook from the subject, one line of the takeaway, and the link to the email's web version.
    • Compressed: for short emails (mindset, case studies). The one idea in 3 to 5 lines, ending with a question they can answer by replying.

    Either way it's one idea and one link. WhatsApp is read on a phone between other things.

  2. 2
    Stay Inside WhatsApp's Limits

    The Centre Does This

    The form saves WhatsApp consent with the contact, and the funnel only sends WhatsApp to people who opted in. STOP replies are pointed at the funnel.

    You Do This

    Complete Meta's business verification.

    Building it by hand instead

    The rules come from Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform:

    • Opt-in first. You may only message people who agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp. Add the tick box to your registration form.
    • Templates outside 24 hours. A message you start (not a reply) must be an approved template. Newsletter twins are the Marketing category.
    • Length. A template body can be up to 1,024 characters. Aim for under 400: a twin is a nudge, not a copy of the email.
    • Replies. When someone replies, a 24-hour window opens and you can answer freely, up to 4,096 characters per message.

    Sources: Meta: template components ↗ · Meta: getting opt-in ↗.

  3. 3
    Turn Each Twin Into a Template

    The Centre Does This

    The wa-templates job submits each approved WhatsApp touch to Meta as a template, checks the review for up to 48 hours and redeploys when approved.

    You Do This

    Give the centre your WhatsApp token, phone number ID and WABA ID.

    Building it by hand instead

    Write the twin with variables for the parts that change, {{1}} for the first name and {{2}} for the link, then submit it in WhatsApp Manager (or your provider's template screen). Meta checks the category. If it says the template will be marked as marketing, that's correct for a newsletter twin: submit it.

    Make the pointer version reusable, so one approved template covers every week and only the variables change.

  4. 4
    Send It an Hour After the Email

    The Centre Does This

    The funnel sends WhatsApp twins for the weekly follow-ups in weeks 1 and 2 to opted-in contacts, on its 5-minute scheduler. Later weeks are not automated by the centre.

    Building it by hand instead

    Schedule the twin an hour after the email lands, never at the same minute, so the WhatsApp reminds them rather than competes. Use your WhatsApp Business provider's broadcast or campaign screen, or the follow-up runner from the starter CRM (it only sends to contacts with WhatsApp consent). Replies come to a real person and count as engagement in your CRM.
