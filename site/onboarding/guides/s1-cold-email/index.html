Cold Email

Domains, inboxes, DNS, warm-up, a verified list and a five-step thread that stops on reply.

1The Full Setup
2The Task

Build a cold email channel that reaches inboxes, not spam folders. You'll buy separate outreach domains, create inboxes on them, prove they're authenticated, warm them up for 2 to 3 weeks, and send a five-step thread from Instantly to a verified list. You're done when every domain passes all three checkers, every inbox shows warm-up on, and a test email lands in a real primary inbox.

3Step by Step
  1. 1
    Buy Separate Outreach Domains and Move Them to Cloudflare

    Never send cold email from your main domain. If it gets flagged, your normal email stops arriving too.

    1. Write 2 branded names and 5 anonymised ones (topic + format). For a SaaS company: onboardingsessions.com, onboardingroom.com.
    2. On Namecheap, open Domains → Domain Name Search and switch to Beast Mode, its bulk search. Any registrar with bulk search works.
    3. Paste all seven at once.
    4. Untick Show Premiums and tick Hide Unavailable, then search.
    5. Add your shortlist to the cart. Confirm it with the owner before you pay.
    6. Move each domain's DNS to Cloudflare, so every record in steps 2 to 6 goes in one place. In Cloudflare, open Domains → Onboard a domain, enter the domain, pick the Free plan and continue until it shows two nameservers.
    7. In Namecheap, open Domain List → Manage for that domain. Under Nameservers, choose Custom DNS, paste the two Cloudflare nameservers and click the green tick.
    8. Wait until Cloudflare shows the domain as Active. It is often under an hour and can take up to 24.
  2. 2
    Create the Inboxes

    Each domain gets 2 or 3 mailboxes on Google Workspace. First names read best as usernames.

    Doing it yourself (needs a paid Google Workspace plan; every mailbox is a paid seat):

    1. In admin.google.com ↗, open Account → Domains → Manage domains → Add a domain. Enter the domain, choose Secondary domain and click Add domain & start verification.
    2. Google shows a TXT verification record. Add it in Cloudflare (DNS → Records → Add record, type TXT, name @), then go back and click Verify.
    3. On Manage domains, click Activate Gmail next to the domain. The MX record it asks for is the one in step 3.
    4. Open Directory → Users → Add new user and create 2 or 3 users. Pick the new domain in the dropdown after the @.
    5. Repeat for every domain.

    Using an inbox provider (Instantly's done-for-you setup or similar): send the domains, the usernames per domain, registrar access and your Instantly login in one go. Create the Instantly account first; their form asks for it.

    Tip If a provider did the setup, change every password you shared once they finish.

  3. 3
    Add the Four DNS Records

    DNS records prove to inbox providers that your mail is really from you. In Cloudflare, open the domain, then DNS → Records → Add record.

    • MX (1 record, name @): SMTP.GOOGLE.COM, priority 1. This is Google's current setting for new Workspace domains. If a domain already has the older five-record set (ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM plus ALT1 to ALT4), it still works; don't mix the two.
    • SPF (TXT, name @): v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
    • DKIM (TXT, name google._domainkey): the key from the next step.
    • DMARC (TXT, name _dmarc): v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:{you}; ruf=mailto:{you}; fo=1

    Delete any other MX records first. Keep exactly one SPF record: two break authentication without any error message.

  4. 4
    Generate the DKIM Key in Google
    1. In the Google Admin console, open Apps → Google Workspace → Gmail → Authenticate email.
    2. Pick the domain under Selected domain and click Generate new record. Keep the key length at 2048 and the selector as google.
    3. Copy the TXT record value into the DKIM record in Cloudflare (name google._domainkey).
    4. Come back and click Start authentication. The status changes to *Authenticating email*; if it errors, the record hasn't spread yet, so wait and click again.

    Repeat for every domain.

  5. 5
    Check Every Domain in Three Free Tools

    Check every domain before anything sends:

    1. Google Admin Toolbox ↗ checks MX, SPF and DKIM in one go.
    2. MXToolbox ↗ → Domain Health gives a full report.
    3. DNS Checker ↗ shows whether the records have spread worldwide.

    A pass looks like this: SPF present, DKIM valid, DMARC found, MX pointing at Google, and nothing listed twice. If DKIM fails, generate a new key, replace the record and click Start authentication again.

  6. 6
    Connect the Inboxes, Start Warm-Up and Add Tracking

    Warm-up exchanges a few friendly emails a day so inbox providers learn to trust a new account.

    1. In Instantly, open Email Accounts → Add New → Google and connect each inbox (skip this if a provider already did). Every inbox should then say *Connected*.
    2. Switch Warmup on for every inbox. Leave it on after campaigns go live.
    3. Set each inbox's Daily limit to 20. Raise it to 50 once warm-up looks clean.
    4. Set the tracking domain on each inbox: Email Accounts → the account → Settings, tick Enable custom tracking domain. In Cloudflare, add the CNAME it shows (host inst, value prox.itrackly.com) and set its Proxy status to DNS only; a proxied record fails the check. Back in Instantly, enter inst.yourdomain.com, click Check Status and save once it reads verified. Until then, don't quote click numbers.

    New inboxes need 2 to 3 weeks before they join a live campaign.

  7. 7
    Clean and Verify the List

    Export the CSV from Apollo or your data vendor. Work on a copy; keep the original as your receipt.

    1. Check the row count matches the invoice.
    2. Add a helper column with =LOWER(TRIM(<email cell>)), pointing at your email column, and remove duplicates with Data → Data cleanup → Remove duplicates.
    3. Delete rows with no email or no first name.
    4. Verify every address with a verifier (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce or similar) and keep only confirmed ones. Unknown or accept-all is not a yes. Instantly can also verify after the import in step 8.
    5. Remove do-not-contact, already contacted, past registrants, bounces and unsubscribes.
    Capture Needed

    The cleaned sheet: the lower-cased email helper column, Data → Data cleanup → Remove duplicates, and the verifier's result column filtered to valid.

  8. 8
    Build the Campaign and Import the Leads
    1. In Instantly, open Campaigns → Create new campaign and name it after the event and channel.
    2. Open Leads → Import → Upload CSV.
    3. Map every column carefully: email, first name, company.
    4. Not verified yet? On the campaign's Leads tab, select the leads and click Verify leads, then remove any that don't come back valid.

    A wrong mapping personalises every email wrongly, unseen until it sends.

  9. 9
    Write the Five-Step Thread

    Open the Sequences tab and write five steps that send on day 0, 3, 6, 9 and 13 (waits of 3, 3, 3 and 4 days).

    • Only step 1 has a subject line. Steps 2 to 5 have none, so they arrive as replies in the same thread.
    • Put an unsubscribe line in every step.
    • Use only tokens Instantly understands: {{firstName}} and {{companyName}}.
    • If a name is missing, drop it from the sentence instead of printing a placeholder.
    • No em dashes. Every link carries utm_source=email.
  10. 10
    Set the Schedule and the Campaign Limit
    1. Under Accounts inside the campaign, pick the inboxes that will send it.
    2. Under Schedule, send Monday to Friday, in business hours in your audience's time zone.
    3. Under Options, set the campaign's daily limit and check Stop on reply is on.

    The campaign's own limit wins: 100 across five inboxes that allow 50 each sends 100 a day, not 250.

  11. 11
    Send a Test, Pass the Gate, Then Launch

    Send yourself a test. Check the tokens filled in and it landed in the primary inbox, not spam.

    Then run the pre-launch gate: warmed inboxes, a verified list with no overlap, copy read inside the tool, unsubscribe on every step, tagged links, and a booking link you've opened in a real browser that shows open slots.

    Instantly creates campaigns stopped. Nothing goes out until you press Launch.

  12. 12
    Handle Replies and Bounces Every Day

    Each day, check sent, opens, replies and bounces in Analytics, and any inbox showing an error or paused warm-up in Email Accounts.

    • An interested reply: answer it from the Unibox and send the registration or booking link. Stop on reply ends their sequence.
    • A bounce or unsubscribe: add them to your do-not-contact list and block them in every tool, not just Instantly.
    • Deliverability slipping: pause. Don't push through.

    Then stamp the send date in column A of your master sheet.
