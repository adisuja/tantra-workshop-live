Social Media Content

Cut one pillar into clips, 60-second videos and quotes, batch them and post every 3 days.

2The Task

Turn one pillar piece into a batch of social posts without writing anything from scratch. Plan the batch in Notion, schedule it in Metricool and read the results in Google Analytics. You're done when the batch is scheduled before the week starts, with a post about your event every 3 days, and every link carries utm_source=social.

How This Changes by Mechanism

Posts are dated and point at registration. Post to the event every 3 days and refresh the date before the next cycle.

3Step by Step
  1. 1
    Cut the Pillar Into Five Pieces

    Everything is cut from one pillar, so a week of posts takes one sitting instead of seven.

    1. From a recording: in Descript, create a project and import the recording. CapCut works too.
    2. In the script, select the words of one of your five best moments, 2 to 4 minutes each. Pick moments that answer a real objection or show the method working.
    3. Click Export, open the Video tab, set Destination to Local export and Export options to Current selection, then click Export. Repeat for each moment.
    4. From a blog post: make five quote cards and one carousel instead.

    Descript's free plan exports at up to 720p, with a watermark.

    Example, for a SaaS company: clip 1 the problem, clip 2 the method, clip 3 the worked example, clip 4 the mistake to avoid, clip 5 the invite.

  2. 2
    Make 60-Second Versions

    Short vertical videos reach people who won't watch a 3-minute clip.

    1. In the same editor, trim each clip to 60 seconds.
    2. Click Aspect ratio & video settings at the top left of the scene editor and choose Portrait.
    3. Open Captions in the right sidebar, set Apply to to All scenes and click a caption style, so the video works with the sound off.

    From the second cycle on, only make short versions of the clips that drove clicks (step 7).

  3. 3
    Pull Quotes and Graphics

    Single lines in your own words are the cheapest posts you'll make.

    1. Lift lines word for word from the pillar.
    2. In Canva, drop each into one quote-card template, so every card looks like yours.
    3. Turn one into a poll if it splits opinion.
    Capture Needed

    Canva editor with a quote-card template and an anonymised quote.

    A real screenshot goes here. Until then, follow the words above.

  4. 4
    Plan the Batch in Notion

    Posting one at a time on the day is how the channel quietly stops. Plan in Notion, schedule in Metricool.

    1. If you have no content calendar yet, sign in to Notion (a free account works), open the Content Calendar template ↗ and add it to your workspace.
    2. Add every clip, video and quote as a card in that calendar.
    3. Give each card a date and a platform.
    4. Plan a post about your event every 3 days until the session.
  5. 5
    Tag Every Link

    Without a tag, no post can be traced to a registration.

    1. Open Google's Campaign URL Builder ↗.
    2. Paste your registration page address into website URL. The field is required.
    3. Enter source social (or the platform name), medium organic, campaign {event_slug} and a short content label per post (for example clip2).
    4. Paste the tagged link into every post that links to your page. The content label is how step 7 tells the posts apart.
  6. 6
    Schedule the Batch in Metricool

    The Centre Does This

    The centre schedules up to 12 approved posts in Metricool for Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 09:30, with optional images made for you.

    You Do This

    Give the centre your Metricool token, user ID, blog ID and a start date.

    Building it by hand instead

    Scheduling isn't sending: you can still edit until the scheduled time. Once live, the post is public.

    LinkedIn needs a paid Metricool plan. The free plan covers 1 brand and caps posts at 20 a month.

    1. Create a Metricool account. Click your profile picture, then Connections, pick your brand, choose each network and authorise it.
    2. Open Planning and click Create post, or click a slot in the calendar.
    3. Choose the profiles, then copy in the text, media and tagged link from one Notion card. Each card is entered by hand.
    4. Set the date and time, check the preview, then click Schedule.
    5. Repeat for every card, and read each post once more before the week starts.
  7. 7
    Read Clicks, Not Likes

    The Centre Does This

    The dashboard syncs your Metricool numbers every hour.

    Building it by hand instead

    Likes and comments aren't traffic. Clicks to your registration page decide what you cut next. GA4 must already be on these pages (Stage 2 guide, step 7).

    1. Each week, open Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition in Google Analytics. Change the table's first column from the channel group to Session source / medium, then type social in the search box above the table.
    2. Click the + next to the column name and add Session manual ad content. It shows the content label from step 5.
    3. The posts with the most sessions on your registration page are your winners. Ignore likes and comments.
    4. Make 60-second versions of the winning clips, cut the next pillar around those topics, and send the best ones to the newsletter.

    Read by Session source / medium, not by channel group. GA4's channel groups can file medium organic under Organic Search, so the Organic Social row may miss these posts.
