Help · Billing

Plans, pricing, and managing your subscription

Compare Tantra plans, choose monthly or yearly billing, review your payment method, and download invoices.

beginner · 5 min read · Updated Aug 13, 2026

Overview#

Use Billing to see your current plan, compare monthly and yearly options, activate channel add-ons, review the card on file, and download past invoices.

Plans and prices#

PlanPriceHow to choose it
Free$0Included when you create an account
Pro monthly$99 per monthSelect Monthly, then Choose plan
Pro yearly$83 per month, billed as $999 once per yearSelect Yearly, then Choose plan
EnterpriseCustomSelect Contact sales

The yearly price is charged as one annual payment, not as twelve monthly payments.

LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Telegram are optional, separate $10-per-month add-ons. See Activate and manage channel add-ons.

Choose a plan#

  1. Open Billing.
  2. Select the Plans tab.
  3. Choose Monthly or Yearly.
  4. Review the plan cards. Your current option is marked Current Plan.
  5. Select Choose plan on the option you want.
  6. Complete the secure checkout.
  7. Return to Billing and confirm that the current subscription card shows the new plan and billing date.

The current subscription card shows:

  • Your plan name and price.
  • Your next billing date, or the date your access ends after cancellation.
  • A summary of the payment card on file.
  • Change plan, Cancel Subscription, or Keep subscription, depending on your current status.

Manage channel add-ons#

Select Add-ons to activate or cancel LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Telegram individually. Each one is billed separately from your main plan.

Review or change your payment method#

Select Payment Method to see the current card and when it was last charged. Tantra does not display or store your full card number.

To change the card on file, contact support. The team will send you a secure update link.

Download an invoice#

  1. Open Billing.
  2. Select Billing History.
  3. Find the payment by date, amount, status, or description.
  4. Select Download to open its invoice.

What happens after checkout#

Your plan normally updates shortly after checkout. If the current subscription card still shows the old plan, wait a moment and refresh the page.

Once the paid plan is active, you can return to the rest of Tantra. If checkout was not completed, choose the plan again from the Plans tab.

Troubleshooting#

SymptomFix
A page keeps returning you to BillingConfirm that the current subscription card shows an active paid plan
The old plan still appears after checkoutWait a moment, then refresh Billing
You cannot change the card in the Payment Method tabContact support for a secure update link
An invoice does not openTry Download again shortly; contact support if it continues
A channel is unavailable after buying ProActivate that channel separately under Add-ons

FAQ#

Is yearly billing charged monthly?#

No. The yearly Pro option is shown as $83 per month for comparison, but $999 is charged once per year.

Are channel add-ons included with Pro?#

No. LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Telegram are separate $10-per-month add-ons.

Where can I find invoices?#

Open Billing History and select Download beside the payment.

How do I change my card?#

Contact support and ask for a secure payment-method update link.
