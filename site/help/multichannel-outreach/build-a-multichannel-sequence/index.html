Build a multichannel outreach sequence

Combine email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Telegram steps in one Tantra outreach sequence.

intermediate · 7 min read · Updated Aug 13, 2026

Overview#

A Tantra outreach sequence can combine email with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Telegram actions. You can also create a sequence that uses only non-email channels.

Available actions are:

ChannelActions
EmailSend email
LinkedInVisit profile, send connection request, send message
WhatsAppSend message
TelegramSend message

Before you start#

  • Activate the add-on for every non-email channel you plan to use.
  • Connect at least one ready account for each channel.
  • Put the necessary identity on each contact record: an email address for email, a LinkedIn profile for LinkedIn, a phone number for WhatsApp, and an existing Telegram identity for Telegram.
  • Add the contacts to a list.

Build the sequence#

  1. Open Campaigns and create an Email Outreach campaign.
  2. Name the campaign and choose the contact list.
  3. In Senders, select the accounts the campaign may send from. Email steps need a connected mailbox. Each other channel needs at least one connected account of that type.
  4. In Sequence, edit the first step or select Add step.
  5. Choose a channel and action.
  6. Write the email or plain-text message, where the action needs one.
  7. Set the delay before each follow-up step.
  8. Add, reorder, or remove steps until the journey is complete.
  9. Set the launch time, sending days and hours, timezone, and limits.
  10. Review the campaign and launch it.

If an add-on is not active, that channel is marked Not in your plan and its actions cannot be selected.

Write social messages#

LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Telegram messages are plain text. Use Insert variable to add contact or sender details, including first name, last name, full name, company, job title, website, location, and sender name or company.

Email-only features such as subjects, email signatures, reply threading, and AI personalization do not appear on social steps.

Message typeLimit or behavior
LinkedIn connection noteOptional, up to 300 characters
LinkedIn messageUp to 8,000 characters
WhatsApp messageUp to 4,096 characters
Telegram messageUp to 4,096 characters
LinkedIn profile visitNo message is written

Short, personal messages usually work best even when a channel allows more characters.

Important channel behavior#

LinkedIn#

  • A profile visit sends no message. Contacts without a LinkedIn profile on their record skip the step.
  • A connection request can be sent with or without a note.
  • A later LinkedIn message step runs only after the contact accepts the connection request.

WhatsApp#

  • Messages are plain text, without email-style HTML formatting.
  • The contact needs a usable phone number, including country code.

Telegram#

  • Tantra can message only contacts whose Telegram identity is already on their contact record from an existing conversation.
  • Contacts without a Telegram identity skip the step.

Select account coverage#

The campaign cannot launch unless every channel used in the sequence has a selected account:

  • If any step sends email, select at least one email mailbox.
  • If a step uses LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or Telegram, select at least one ready account for that specific channel.
  • An all-social sequence does not require an email mailbox.

If no account is ready, use the link in Senders to open the channel connector, connect an account, then return to the campaign.

Timing and launch#

The first step starts with no delay. Later steps can wait up to 365 days. The sequence runs in its selected timezone and sending window.

If you schedule the launch, the chosen date and time are interpreted in the campaign timezone. Changing the timezone keeps the displayed clock time but changes when that moment occurs elsewhere.

Troubleshooting#

SymptomFix
A channel says Not in your planActivate that channel under Billing > Add-ons
The campaign says no account is selectedReturn to Senders and choose a ready account for every channel used
No account is available to selectConnect it under Connectors, or wait for a syncing account to become ready
A LinkedIn profile visit is skippedAdd the contact's LinkedIn profile to the contact record
A LinkedIn message after a connection request is waitingThe message runs only after the request is accepted
A WhatsApp step is skippedAdd a valid phone number with country code to the contact
A Telegram step is skippedThe contact needs an existing Telegram identity on their contact record
A message will not saveShorten it to the character limit shown beside the editor
