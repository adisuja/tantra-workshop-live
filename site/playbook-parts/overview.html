The 3 Days at a Glance: From a Blank Page to a Self-Running System

Three days take you from a blank page to a revenue system an agent runs for you: Day 1 decides, Day 2 builds, Day 3 hands it to the agent and proves it works. This chart shows how the days connect and the one task you must start early. The chapters below explain every step; you carry them out one at a time in your onboarding centre, or through each day's questions.

The Daily Rhythm: Watch → Fill → Get → Do

Every day of the workshop runs the same four beats:

  1. Watch the live session. Teaching plus Q&A, live. No recording-only days: you build alongside everyone else.
  2. Fill the day's questions, after the teaching. The first 30 minutes of each session are teaching. Then you open the day's questions and your onboarding centre tasks and work through them, with sample answers beside every question so you always know what good looks like.
  3. Get your generated output. Your answers run through an AI workflow and come back the same day as a PDF and a live link, by WhatsApp and email. Your prompt pack also sits in that day's step of your onboarding centre.
  4. Do the build, one task at a time. Your onboarding centre (or the day's questions) shows one task, and the next opens only when it is done properly; the playbook explains every step. Each day starts from the one before, so skipping a day is the single biggest reason people fall behind.

The amber bar is the trap. Domain warm-up is the only task with a hard external wait, and it's the one people start last. Begin it on day one and it's ready when your funnel is; begin it after the workshop ends and you'll be sitting on a finished funnel with nowhere to send from.
Summary: The Lessons in This Section
  1. Every day runs the same four beats: watch the session, fill the survey, get your generated output, do the homework.
  2. Day 1 decides: the offer, the buyer, the prospect list, the funnel and the event date.
  3. Day 2 builds: every page, sequence, script and automation, stage by stage.
  4. Day 3 runs and measures: an AI agent takes over operations, a dashboard fills itself, and a weekly experiment loop begins.
  5. The playbook explains every step. You do the steps one at a time in your onboarding centre, or through each day's questions.
  6. Start domain warm-up on Day 1. It is the only task with a fixed 2 to 3 week wait.
