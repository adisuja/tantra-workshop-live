Integrations · Outreach

Continue existing Telegram conversations from Tantra.

Link Telegram by QR code and add message steps for contacts who already have a Telegram identity connected to their Tantra record.

Availablepaid-add-on
Activate or connect TelegramRead Setup Guide

What You Get

QR-based connection

Link your Telegram account with a QR code from Telegram Devices.

Plain-text message steps

Add plain-text Telegram message steps to a sequence.

Daily and weekly limits

Per-account daily and weekly message limits apply to Telegram activity.

Account sync and reconnect

See account sync and reconnect states for a connected Telegram account.

Unified inbox and webhooks

Replies land in the unified inbox, and sequence-linked replies can trigger a webhook.

Graceful skip for missing identities

Contacts without a connected Telegram identity are skipped instead of failing the step.

How It Works

  1. 1
    Activate the add-on
  2. 2
    Scan a QR code from Telegram Devices
  3. 3
    Sync conversations
  4. 4
    Match existing identities
  5. 5
    Add a Telegram step

Supported Actions and Data

Tantra Can Do

  • Send plain-text Telegram messages to contacts with a connected identity
  • Add Telegram steps alongside email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp in one sequence

Tantra Receives

  • Sequence-linked Telegram replies in the unified inbox
  • Account sync and reconnect status

Requirements and Permissions

  • An active Tantra plan with the Telegram add-on
  • A Telegram account reachable to scan a QR code
  • Contacts with an existing Telegram identity already connected to their record

Good to Know

  • Tantra cannot start a Telegram conversation with an arbitrary username or phone number. An existing conversation or identity is required.
  • Contacts without an eligible Telegram identity are skipped, not failed.
  • Telegram is a separate channel add-on at $10 per month, billed on top of your plan.
