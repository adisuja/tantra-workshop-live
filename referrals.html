Your Links

Share either link as it is. The free link opens the free workshop page, the paid link the paid one. Both carry your code, and the cohort tag updates itself every week. To compare posts, add a label to your code, for example adi-p014; it still counts as yours.

Who Gets Credit

A visit counts once per browser, for the first referral link it arrived through. A registration or sale is credited to the code on the person's first registration, or, when that registration carried no code, the code they paid through. A later click on someone else's link doesn't move it. Each week is the cohort the person signed up for.

Commission

A share of what the buyer actually paid, tax included, with refunds removed. Payouts follow Adi's schedule.
